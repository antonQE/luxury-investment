# EPIC-08: Team, offices and partners

- Status: Backlog
- Priority: P1
- Source: 01 §2, §5; 04 ADMIN-003; 06 CONTENT-003; 11 A-04

## Goal

Admins manage team members (agents, staff, lawyers, external partners), offices and partner logos; the public site shows team profiles, offices with maps and the partner carousel.

## Scope

- In: team member registry with public and internal profile, kind, qualifications (assigned only to the person who holds them), markets, languages, contacts, order and visibility; link to `User` when the member has admin access; offices with address, map, contacts, hours; partners with logo, link, order, on/off; public team, office and contact pages; partner carousel.
- Out: performance reports per agent (EPIC-11, EPIC-18).
